FAQ

Quick answers about visiting the barn

Is the barn wheelchair accessible?
Yes! Our barn is fully accessible with paved pathways throughout the grounds. The main barn floor is flat and wide for easy navigation.
Can I book a wedding or private party?
Absolutely! Our pavilion is available for birthday parties, bridal showers, rehearsal dinners, and corporate events. Message us at (570) 583-2305 or fill out the contact form below.
Do you have the petting farm and café at the same location?
Yes! All Experiences are under one roof at 1584 S Delaware Drive. Barn Brew Coffee, the mercantile, petting farm, and pavilion are all inside our restored 1890s barn.
How do I pay at the barn?
We accept Square, CashApp, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover & JCB. All online orders are processed securely through Square.
Are online orders returnable?
All sales are final — no returns or refunds. If your order arrives damaged, contact us within 3 days at allaspectsrecycled@gmail.com and we'll work with the carrier to resolve it.
