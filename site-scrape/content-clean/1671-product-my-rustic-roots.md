Experience the tradition of real sourdough baking with our My Rustic Roots Original Sourdough Starter. Made using only organic, non-GMO flour and water, this carefully cultivated culture allows home bakers to create authentic, naturally fermented sourdough bread right in their own kitchens.

Our starter is gently freeze-dried to preserve the living culture, allowing it to remain shelf-stable until it is ready to be activated. Once rehydrated and fed, the starter becomes a living culture that can be maintained indefinitely and used to bake artisan sourdough bread, pizza crust, waffles, pancakes, and more.

Each 12-gram packet contains enough culture to begin a new sourdough starter that grows stronger with regular feedings. With just flour, water, and time, customers can enjoy the flavor, texture, and tradition of homemade sourdough baking.

Sourdough baking continues to grow in popularity among home bakers, making this a strong specialty baking product for kitchen shops, gift stores, and gourmet retailers.
