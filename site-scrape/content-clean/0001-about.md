### Our Story
## All Aspects Restored & Recycled is a family-owned, unique shop on Route 611 in the Village of Stone Church in Upper Mount Bethel, PA. Our vintage store has a growing and forever changing inventory of unusual finds, antiques, reclaimed items, refinished furniture, home decor, primitives and cool old stuff! We are a Premier Retailer for Dixie Belle Mineral Chalk Paint as well as a Retailer for Wise Owl Chalk Synthesis Paint for the DIY-ers!
## We are open Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 10-5, as well as open by appointment, Sunday-Wednesday.
