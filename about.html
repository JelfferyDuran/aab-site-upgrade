All Aspects Restored & Recycled

All Aspects Restored & Recycled is a family-owned, unique shop on Route 611 in the Village of Stone Church, Upper Mount Bethel, PA.

Our vintage store carries a growing and forever-changing inventory of unusual finds: antiques, reclaimed items, refinished furniture, home decor, primitives, and cool old stuff.

Every visit is a little different — that's the fun of it.
